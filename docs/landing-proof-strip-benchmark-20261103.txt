PPWG landing-layout benchmark and compact proof strip — 2026-11-03

Scope: public clinic acquisition-entry pages, inspected at 1440 x 1000.
Their paid-campaign destinations and conversion results are not known.
Observations support layout choices, not claims of conversion superiority.

Benchmarks:
1. Ketamine Clinics Los Angeles — https://ketamineclinics.com/
   Large visual hero, short headline, prominent consultation action. Public
   page content introduces the clinic and its experience before treatment
   choices. Rendered hero consult button observed near y=827 in this viewport.
2. NeuroRelief — https://neuroreliefketamine.com/
   Large image-led hero with call/consultation actions. Google review widget
   precedes a detailed clinic/program explanation. Observed review widget
   height approximately 278px at y=1275; this is a full testimonial widget,
   not the compact strip requested for PPWG.
3. Nushama — https://nushama.com/
   Image/copy hero with a consultation action, followed early by the medical
   team. Testimonials occur later. Observed testimonial widget height ~232px.
   Clinic environment and clinician identity supply credibility near the top.

Only structure was benchmarked. Competitor medical efficacy, safety, insurance
and outcome claims were not verified or copied. Some animated headings were
blank before scrolling; exact downstream pixel positions are approximate.
Trackers and submissions were blocked during browser inspection.

PPWG baseline, measured before this change:
- Desktop 1440px: hero 434px; reviews 336px; clinic/experience section 885px.
- Mobile 390px: hero 530px; reviews 372px; clinic/experience section 1252px.

Implementation:
- Desktop hero at >=1024px gets a 540px minimum height; its copy remains short.
  Smaller-screen hero sizing is retained.
- Four verified reviews and their exact original Google links are retained.
  Stars/name/source share a line; large card surfaces and excess padding are
  removed; manual carousel controls share the strip. No text is line-clamped.
- Gold 26px stars, all four excerpts, swipe, keyboard, reduced motion and
  accessible status updates are preserved. No autoplay.
- Final order: hero -> compact reviews -> blue trust banner -> clinic/
  physician section -> two treatment cards -> process -> existing pricing.
- Clinic section retains all clinical text and credentials; heading and spacing
  are more compact. The decorative lifestyle image is hidden on mobile, while
  the physician headshot remains visible. No new clinical or outcome claim.
- No ad, budget, GA4/GTM, private form or private-handoff changes.

Interpretation:
Showing the clinic and physician before asking visitors to choose a treatment
path is a reasonable confidence-building sequence. Keeping that section concise
and retaining direct header navigation limits the extra distance to the options.
A conversion or Quality Score improvement cannot be claimed from layout alone.
